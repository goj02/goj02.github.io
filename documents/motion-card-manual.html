01

Product overview

Motion Cards are 3D-printed versions of traditional trading cards that offer a tactile experience based on the artwork of the original trading card. Assembly of the Motion Card generally requires no tools, and requires no particular training.

03

Safety information

Suitable for ages 6 and up. Keep away from small children due to choking hazards. Avoid excessive force during assembly to prevent breakage of components.

  • Inspect all parts before assembly. Do not use any component that is cracked, warped, incomplete, or visibly damaged.
  • Keep away from excessive heat, open flame, and direct sunlight if the material is heat-sensitive.
  • Use only light hand pressure during assembly.
  • Do not force parts together.

04

Assembly instructions

  1. Lay the Card Back on a flat surface, then insert the Interface Connectors into the Card Back notches.
  2. Insert the Secondary Gear through the hole in the Card Back with the cross end facing downward. Connect the Gear Handle to the Secondary Gear by lining up the cross notches.
  3. Insert the Character Gear into the main depression adjacent to the Secondary Gear.
  4. Insert the Character Plate into the Character Gear by lining up the peg on the back of the Character Plate with the notch in the Character Gear
  5. Attach the Card Front to the Card Back by aligning the Interface Connectors and pressing firmly.
  6. Verify the Motion Card is assembled by turning the Gear Handle and observing the movement of the Character Plate.

05

Final inspection

  • Check that all joints are fully engaged.
  • Rotate the Gear Handle in both directions to ensure smooth movement.
  • Gently test stability before use.

06

Maintenance

  • Clean with a dry microfibercloth.
  • If needed, use mild soap and water, then dry immediately.
  • Avoid abrasive cleaners or solvents.
  • Store in a cool, dry place.
